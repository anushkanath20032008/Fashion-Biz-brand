## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-fashion digital flagship for contemporary Indian occasionwear. Rejecting the typical industry cliches of heavy filigree, ubiquitous gold leaf, ornate palace tapestries, and bridal excess, it articulates **presence without excess**. It is conceived around the dynamic interplay of **The Modern Muse** (effortless elegance, tactile sensuality, fluid draping) and **The Icon** (sharp architectural lines, uncompromising precision, statuesque confidence).

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Minimalism & Architectural High-Fashion:** Expansive negative space, razor-sharp structural alignments, and disciplined grid divisions reminiscent of high-end editorial publications and Parisian couture houses.
- **Atmospheric Restraint:** A warm, tactile foundation grounded in warm ivory and deep espresso, punctuated by deliberate moments of rich oxblood and ink black. 
- **Emotional Resonance:** Quiet confidence, undeniable presence, and composed luxury. The interface recedes completely to let silhouette, drape, and texture command the viewer's attention.

## Colors

The palette is intentionally limited to five disciplined tones. Luxury is expressed through extreme chromatic restraint: never use all five colors within a single view. Most compositions rely on Warm Ivory as the canvas, Ink Black for crisp typography and primary elements, and a single intentional accent.

- **Warm Ivory (`#F4EFE7`):** The primary canvas and surface tone. Soft, warm-neutral, and editorial. Replaces stark, sterile digital white to echo high-grade unbleached cotton paper and raw silk.
- **Ink Black (`#151515`):** The primary anchor. Delivers graphic authority in typography, borders, and primary CTA surfaces. Sharp, dense, and uncompromising.
- **Champagne (`#D8C8AE`):** The secondary subtle accent. Used for hairline dividers, understated tags, packaging nods, and secondary hover states. Never rendered as shimmering metallic glitter; it remains a matte, chalky tone.
- **Deep Espresso (`#3A2924`):** Supporting dark tone. Used for immersive campaign backdrops, alternating rich section panels, and product framing.
- **Oxblood (`#641F28`):** The signature fashion accent. Reserved strictly for curated editorial highlights, active selection indicators, and signature interactive badges.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast editorial drama with Swiss utilitarian clarity.

- **Editorial Serifs (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for high-impact brand statements, hero entrances, section titles, and product naming. Typeset primarily in regular weight (`400`) to highlight clean serifs, vertical stress, and graceful strokes. Avoid faux-bolding or italics except for isolated editorial citations.
- **Modern Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Anchors functional UI, micro-copy, specifications, pricing, and navigations. Its geometric structure and balanced proportions guarantee crisp legibility against dark imagery or soft paper tones.
- **Letter Spacing & Casing Rules:**
  - Micro-metadata, category headers, and button labels employ uppercase styling with generous tracking (`0.14em` to `0.18em`).
  - Headlines and product names remain in sentence case or natural title case. Never typeset serif headlines in all-caps.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts favor dramatic vertical proportion, echoing the silhouettes of sarees and shararas.

### Grid & Canvas Structure
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `4rem` outer margins and `2.5rem` gutters. Product showcases leverage asymmetric splits (e.g., 7 columns for vertical high-fashion imagery paired with 5 columns for typography and sticky action bars).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `1.25rem` outer canvas padding and `1rem` column gaps. Full-bleed vertical image framing with uninterrupted scroll cascades.

### Architectural Whitespace Rhythm
- Negative space is treated as an active luxury material. Generous vertical breathing room (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) isolates garments to recreate the exclusivity of a private atelier viewing.
- Grid dividers are sparse, constructed exclusively with 1px hairline strokes in Champagne or low-opacity Ink Black.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic editorial and architectural presence, this design system rejects heavy drop shadows, synthetic neon blurs, and pillowy neo-morphic depth.

- **Primary Elevation Model: Planar Stacking & Hairline Tension:** Depth is expressed horizontally and vertically through layered physical planes. Warm Ivory resting on Deep Espresso creates dramatic tone-on-tone hierarchy without faux lighting.
- **Low-Contrast Outlines & Monolithic Frames:** Structural boundaries rely on ultra-fine 1px boundaries (`rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.12)` on light surfaces, `rgba(216, 200, 174, 0.25)` on dark surfaces).
- **Transient Depth (Overlays & Menus):** When navigation trays or cart drawers slide into view, they enter over an Ink Black backdrop veil tinted at 60% opacity (`rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.6)`). The surface itself is solid Warm Ivory or Deep Espresso with a singular, whisper-thin ambient falloff: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system is strictly **Sharp (`0`)**.

- **Zero Radius:** All corners on buttons, inputs, product cards, dialogs, media framing, and panels are rendered with `0px` radius.
- **Architectural Rationale:** Razor-sharp geometry aligns with the structured shoulders, sculpted necklines, and clean pleat falls of the garments. Rounded corners soften the tension and evoke casual tech interfaces, whereas crisp right angles frame luxury fashion photography with museum-grade precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Solid Ink Black):** Sharp rectangle (`0px` border-radius), background `#151515`, text `#F4EFE7`, uppercase `label-caps-wide`, padding `1.125rem 2.25rem`. Hover smoothly shifts background to `#3A2924` with zero layout shift.
- **Secondary Action (Hairline Frame):** Background transparent, 1px border `#151515`, text `#151515`. On hover, fills solid `#151515` with text transitioning to `#F4EFE7`.
- **Editorial Text Action:** Pure text link in `label-caps-wide` with a persistent 1px underline positioned 4px below baseline. Hover translates the line subtly or deepens color from `#151515` to `#641F28`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Edge-to-edge vertical framing (aspect ratio 4:5 or 2:3). Unbroken crisp lines.
- **Imagery:** Neutral studio or architectural backdrop. Hover triggers a cross-dissolve to a fabric texture close-up (2.0x) or dynamic drape movement.
- **Meta Treatment:** Stacked beneath the image with breathing room: Product name in `title-editorial` (`Playfair Display`), silhouette descriptor in `body-sm`, and price in `price-editorial` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`). No cluttered floating badges.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Fields:** Minimalist bottom-line borders (1px `#151515` or `#D8C8AE`) or full sharp rectangular bounding boxes on `#F4EFE7` with 1px border `rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.2)`. Focus state sharpens the outline to 1px solid `#151515` with no outer glow rings.
- **Labels:** Floated top in `label-caps-wide` with generous tracking. Error states replace the border with `#641F28`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- **Checkboxes:** 16px sharp square. Unselected: 1px border `#151515`. Selected: solid `#151515` fill housing a crisp ivory checkmark.
- **Size & Drape Selectors:** Sharp rectangular chips (`44px` height). Default has 1px border in `rgba(21, 21, 21, 0.15)`. Selected state fills solid `#151515` with `#F4EFE7` typography. Unavailable sizes feature a clean diagonal 1px strikethrough.

### Editorial Swatches & Detail Toggles
- **Fabric Swatches:** 32px sharp squares displaying actual high-resolution fabric texture or uniform color (#151515, #3A2924, #641F28). Selected state is framed by an outer 1px hairline separated by 2px white space.
- **Accordion / Spec Drawers:** Hairline borders top and bottom. Clean plus/minus (`+` / `—`) toggle. Smooth vertical expansion revealing verified garment claims, material purity, and drape notes.
